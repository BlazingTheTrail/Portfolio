# Criminal Cases in Los Angeles

## Project Summary

This public-data visualization project explores Los Angeles crime records from 2010. The dashboard organizes the records across several descriptive dimensions, including time of occurrence, location, offense category, and victim attributes.

[Open the Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/xiang.ding/viz/Crime2010LA/DashboardCrimeinLosAngeles)

## Skills Demonstrated

- data collection and preparation;
- SQL-based transformation in MySQL;
- exploratory analysis;
- Tableau dashboard design;
- communication of categorical, temporal, and geographic patterns.

## Technology

- MySQL
- SQL
- DataGrip
- Tableau

## Analytical Scope

The project is descriptive rather than predictive. Its purpose is to make a single year of records easier to explore and compare, not to explain the causes of crime or estimate future risk.

## Interpretation Limitations

- The analysis covers 2010 and should not be interpreted as a current view of Los Angeles.
- Recorded incidents may reflect reporting and administrative practices as well as underlying events.
- Counts should not be compared across neighborhoods without considering population, exposure, and other contextual differences.
- Relationships shown in the dashboard are descriptive and do not establish causation.

## Recommended Future Improvements

- identify and link the exact public-data source;
- document the cleaning and SQL transformation steps;
- add a dashboard preview image;
- summarize three to five verified findings;
- normalize selected measures where appropriate;
- improve accessibility through consistent labels, color contrast, and explanatory tooltips.
